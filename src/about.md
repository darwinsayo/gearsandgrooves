Gears & Grooves is where I write about the two things I can't stop doing: **fixing things** and **making music**.

By day I'm in the garage or the engineering lab, figuring out why something doesn't work and how to make it work better. By night I'm building tracks, and I actually publish them.

Here's what you'll find:

- **Gears**: car builds, repairs, and before-and-afters.
- **Grooves**: my releases and the stories behind them.
- **Engineering Life**: college, projects, and lessons learned.
- **Kuya Talk**: advice for my younger siblings, and for anyone else who's the oldest.

Thanks for stopping by.
