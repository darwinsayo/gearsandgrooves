Every DIY mechanic has a drain plug story. Here's the checklist that keeps me from adding to mine.

1. Warm the engine for a few minutes, then shut it off.
2. Get the right oil and filter for your car (check the owner's manual).
3. Jack it up safely and **use jack stands**.
4. Drain the oil and replace the filter.
5. Reinstall the drain plug with a new crush washer.
6. Fill, check the dipstick, and look for leaks.

> Torque it right, not tight.
